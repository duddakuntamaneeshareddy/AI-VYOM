# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Primary: Shoppers seeking a seamless, AI-assisted e-commerce experience — browsing, discovering, and buying with the help of voice and conversational AI. They expect the product to feel effortless and premium, like a personal stylist embedded in the interface.

Secondary: Retailers managing inventory, adding products, and monitoring orders through a portal that is functional but still beautiful.

## Product Purpose
An AI-powered storefront that feels like a $100k product — where sophisticated AI assistance (voice ordering, chat, real-time recommendations) meets an interface that communicates trust, precision, and premium quality at a glance. Success means a shopper feels confident and excited to interact, and a retailer feels in control without feeling overwhelmed.

## Brand Personality
Bold, precision-engineered, and high-touch. The experience should feel like Vercel met a luxury commercial storefront — hyper-clean, technically impressive, and unmistakably expensive. Not cold, but confident. Not flashy, but striking.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS (glowing orbs, purple gradient soup, floating particles with no purpose)
- Overly minimal / sterile interfaces (plain white, no personality, nothing to remember)
- Cluttered legacy e-commerce grids (Amazon/eBay density)
- Warm-cozy boutique aesthetic (beige, serif heavy, soft and slow)
- Linear — too tool-like, too productivity-focused for a shopping context

## Design Principles
- **Precision over decoration:** Every visual element earns its place. If it doesn't serve clarity or delight, remove it.
- **Dark is home:** The dark theme is the identity, not an afterthought. Design dark-first and ensure light mode is equally premium.
- **AI presence, not AI noise:** The AI assistant should feel like a trusted guide embedded in the experience — surfaced when useful, never announcing itself with visual clichés.
- **$100k signal:** Details communicate quality. Typography, spacing rhythm, micro-interactions, and transitions should all signal craftsmanship.
- **Commerce-grade trust:** Even with bold aesthetics, the interface must feel safe and trustworthy for real purchasing decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum — 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text, 3:1 for large text. Reduced motion alternatives required for all entrance animations. AI voice/chat interactions must remain accessible and screen-reader friendly.
